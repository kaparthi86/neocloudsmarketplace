About Neo Clouds

We bring unused accelerators online — an open marketplace for available GPUs and TPUs at low cost.

The problem

AI demand for compute keeps rising, but a lot of capacity never reaches the market. Idle chips sit across neo-clouds, labs, and colos while teams chase quotes or overpay hyperscalers.

What we’re building

Neo Clouds is an open platform to aggregate unused and underutilized hardware from many providers into one catalog — discoverable by hardware, workload, and price — with APIs builders can use.

  • Unused → online — list idle GPUs and TPUs from diverse providers
  • Low / min cost — transparent rates; early access free to explore
  • Open — open hardware catalog, open APIs, open source

Who it’s for

Providers with spare capacity who want another distribution channel.
Builders who need flexible accelerator access without owning a fleet.

Stage

We are in early access. Demo listings stay simulated. Real providers can connect via the provider console and neo-agent (heartbeat, attest, provision). Payments are still disabled — we would rather be honest than overclaim a paid fleet.
