Privacy Policy

Neo Clouds · neocloudsmarketplace.com

Last updated: September 2026

Neo Clouds is an open GPU marketplace. This policy describes what we collect when you use the site and API.

What we collect

  • Account info: name and email when you register for an API key. The key can be emailed back to that address when outbound mail is configured.
  • Inbound notes: contact and provider-waitlist messages are stored for the operator inbox and, when configured, emailed to the operator.
  • Provider pilot waitlist: name, email, optional company, and hardware inventory you submit on the provider pilot form.
  • Contact form: name, email, intent, and message when you write via Contact.
  • Usage data: reservations you create.
  • Technical logs: IP address, request timestamps, and errors for security and uptime.

What we do not do

  • We do not sell your personal data.
  • We do not run third-party ad trackers on the marketplace UI.

Retention

API keys and usage records are kept while your account is active. You may request deletion by contacting the operator.

Contact

Questions: use the Contact form.
