## Category context, not company proof

Public market research can frame the category. It is not validation of Neo Clouds. If a review packet cites it, keep the publisher and the date on the sentence, and keep it away from any Neo Clouds revenue figure.

Examples a reviewer can check, as category context only:

- Synergy Research Group, 13 Oct 2025, on neocloud revenue approaching about $180B in 2030
- MarketsandMarkets, 27 Mar 2025, on GPUaaS from $8.21B in 2025 to $26.62B in 2030

Those sentences are about the category. They are not Neo Clouds results.
